As a reminder, customer fiat balances vary on a quarter to quarter basis depending on customer activity and we do not manage our business to grow these balances.  Custodial fee revenue was $16 million, down 7% Q/Q. The decline was primarily driven by lower blended average fees as well as lower average crypto prices in Q3 compared to Q2. As shared last quarter, we customize pricing on the Coinbase Prime platform based on customer needs between trading, custody, financing, and other products. As a result, we anticipate that our product- specific fees will vary from quarter to quarter depending on the types of customers onboarded and using the Coinbase Prime suite of products in any particular period. Other subscription and services revenue was $32 million, up 10% Q/Q. The increase was driven by higher Coinbase One revenue, where we continued to see higher paid subscribers, as well as growth in our Prime Financing product.
